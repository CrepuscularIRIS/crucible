## D. Analysis & Interpretation

**D1 — The report's error accounting understates its own headline failure by 6.2× on squared error and 2.5× on deviation.** Report L71 and attack G17 both state the same decomposition: `误差集中在四个反跳项（每项平方误差均值≈9，平均偏差≈3 spike）`.
