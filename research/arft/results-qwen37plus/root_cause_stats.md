# Root-cause pillars × lifecycle stage (HIT counts)

Pillars roll up to the single systemic root cause, **Metacognitive Deficit**.

- **P1 Grounding & Faithfulness** — Disconnect between high-level claims/hypotheses and ground-truth code, data, or logs.
- **P2 Cognitive Depth & Adaptability** — Shallow reasoning/search, passivity in self-review, and inability to re-plan or pivot.
- **P3 Integrity & Alignment** — Metric hacking, shortcut reliance, confirmation bias, overclaiming, and goal drift.
- **P4 Engineering Robustness** — Numerical overflows, unhandled runtime errors, and broken interaction with CLI/OS.
